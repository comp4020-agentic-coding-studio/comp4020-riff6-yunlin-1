## The brief

Starting in week 1, keep a running log of omissions you notice --- in
anything you read, watch, hear, use or make, not only in course
material. Each entry: what's missing, where exactly you found it, and
one to three sentences on what leaving it out cost and what it bought.

By the end of week 5 you should have at least eight entries spanning at
least four different disciplines, and at least one entry from something
that has nothing to do with this course's set reading.

## Submission format

A single Markdown or plain-text file, entries in date order, each headed
with the date you noticed it (not the date you wrote it up). Late
entries backfilled after the fact are obvious and marked accordingly.

## What this is testing

Whether you can find the semester's pattern unprompted, in material
nobody assigned you --- the skill the rest of the course assumes you're
building toward, tested here before it's asked of you at scale.
